## 2. 天气队结构（Sun / Rain / Sand / Hail）

### 2.1 【官方文章】雨天队骨架（含 6 槽）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue37/weather-wars-in-doubles
- **原文摘录**："A basic rain team consists of Politoed, a Grass-type, a check to Mega Charizard Y, a bulky Steel-type, an Intimidate user, and a wildcard. Each of these serve a specific goal in allowing a rain team to function effectively."
- **槽位解读（6 槽）**：① 天气设置手 ② 草系（补队伍水/地弱点）③ **对「最强天气克星」的专向答案** ④ 厚实钢系 ⑤ 威吓手 ⑥ 自由位。
- **适配**：`【跨环境通用】`——槽位**功能**可移植；**人选与数值换 ER 图鉴与口径表**。

### 2.2 【官方文章】PU 天气构筑：四种天气的 Teambuilding Skeleton

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/pu-weather（全文精读）
- **雨队原文**：
  - "Rain is a pretty simple style to build. **First add a Volbeat.** This thing is pretty much essential on any Rain team with Prankster weather setting, a slow U-turn, and solid bulk. Next you'll want a **lead setter**, something to start the game with that ideally can set both Stealth Rock and Rain. … Then you just need a few **rain sweepers** and a **resist slot** and you're good to go!"
  - 骨架句："**Teambuilding Skeleton** — Volbeat + Relicanth + Special Sweeper + Special Sweeper + Physical Sweeper + Filler"
  - 末槽判据："…these Pokémon don't have much defensive synergy, and Grass- and Water-types in particular run through the team. The last slot is typically filled by a Grass-type like Roselia … with the main factor in common simply being an **Electric resist**."
- **槽位解读（6 槽，雨）**：① 恶作剧之心天气中转手（先制求雨 + 慢速折返）② 首发钉子/天气双职手 ③④ 特攻雨天清场手 ×2 ⑤ 物攻清场手 ⑥ 补盲位（**必修：电抗**）。
- **沙队骨架（同页）**：Hippopotas（扬沙 + 隐形岩）+ Stoutland（挖沙提速）+ 草/水双弱点检查 + Magic Guard/Overcoat/沙免疫位 + Filler + Filler。
- **适配**：`【需按 ER 数值校准】`——骨架可用，但 ER 天气增伤 ×1.5、回合 8/12（非无限），**「首发双职手」槽位价值上升**（一次落地兼顾钉子 + 天气）。

### 2.3 【官方文章】天气队构筑三条硬规则

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/dp/articles/sunny_day_guide（经 `_02` §6.4 登记）
- **要点**：① 携带天气招的成员**不超过 3–4 只**；② **必须带除钉**；③ **必须准备反制「对手改天气」**的手段。

### 2.4 【官方文章】「天气归属」是可判定的一维（两代机制相反，须实测）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue20/vgc_effects
- **原文摘录**："The Speed of the Pokemon is critical in determining which weather will be in place at the start of the battle."
- **【社区指南】现代口径（相反）**：https://pokestats.cc/guides/sun-team-guide —— "**Weather priority is determined by activation order — the slower Pokémon's ability activates last, overriding faster ones.** Koraidon (135 Spe) is faster than Kyogre (90 Spe), so when both enter simultaneously, Kyogre's Drizzle overwrites Koraidon's Sun."
- **槽位含义（关键）**：**「谁主导天气」必须写进槽位表**，否则整队可被对手一只天气手废掉；且**速度方向相反会得出完全相反的选人结论**（老口径「快者赢」vs 新口径「慢者赢」）。
- **ER 适配**：`【待实测】`——ER 属第三代内核但有现代改动，归属规则须游戏内实测；07 文档把「天气争夺槽」列为**天气队必备槽位**，并把速度方向标注为**可配置参数**（`wxGrantOrder`）。

### 2.5 【官方文章】天气可服务「从攻到受」的全部流派

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/a-brief-guide-to-weather.3664292/
- **原文摘录**："The weather mechanic is the single most defining factor of the Black and White OU metagame … The wide variety of utilizations of the weather mechanic can be utilized to supplement **every play-style from Offense to Stall**."
- **槽位含义**：同一套天气骨架可铸成**攻向**（清场手为主）或**受向**（沙隐/冰冻之躯 + 消耗），**槽位构成因此分叉**——07 给沙/雪同时列「攻向打手槽」与「受向站场槽」两个可选槽位。

### 2.6 【官方文章】「设置手与受益者抢同一只」的原始取舍

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue38/whether-you-like-it-or-not
- **原文摘录**："Although Hail is not such a viable playstyle at the moment, it may become more common once Aurorus's unreleased ability, Snow Warning, is released. Although, using Snow Warning on Aurorus would cost you the only reason to use Aurorus: Refridgerate Hyper Voice."
- **槽位含义**：**「一槽多职」的取舍**是槽位设计的原生问题；ER 因 **abis 池 n 选 1** 把该取舍**变成战前可切换**（§8.1）。

### 2.7 【改版社区】通用天气队构筑：四系对照 + **6 槽配表** + 五步流程

- **URL**：https://pokemonteambuild.com/blog/how-to-build-a-weather-team-pokemon/（片段核验通过）
- **四系机制表（原文照录要点）**：雨 = Drizzle / Swift Swim 2× / 水招 +50% / 火招 −50%；晴 = Drought / Chlorophyll 2× / 火招 +50% / 水招 −50%；沙 = Sand Stream / Sand Rush 2× / 无增伤 / 岩石特防 +50%；雪 = Snow Warning / Slush Rush 2× / 无增伤 / 冰系防御 +50%。
- **雨队 6 槽样本（原文表）**：1 Pelipper（雨设置手 + U-turn 中转）/ 2 Barraskewda（悠游自如物攻清场）/ 3 Kingdra（悠游自如特攻清场）/ 4 Ferrothorn（钉子 + 草/电抗）/ 5 Zapdos（电免疫 + 草抗）/ 6 Garchomp（地面补盲，管电系威胁）。
- **晴队 6 槽样本（原文表）**：1 Torkoal（晴设置手 + 喷火核弹）/ 2 Venusaur（叶绿素清场）/ 3 Charizard（太阳之力特攻）/ 4 Landorus-T（地面补盲 + 威吓）/ 5 Heatran（钢系 + 引火）/ 6 Cresselia（空间选项 + 耐久）。
- **五步流程（原文步骤）**："1. Choose your weather … 2. **Pick your setter** — reliability matters more than power here. 3. **Add 2 weather abusers** … 4. **Fill 2-3 slots** with Pokémon that cover your weather team's weaknesses. 5. Check coverage … at least 15/18 types offensively. 6. Verify shared weaknesses."
- **原文弱点段**："Include at least one Pokémon that functions **without** weather." / "Cover your weather's obvious weaknesses (**Grass resist on rain teams, Rock resist on sun teams**)." / 反例："**Stack 4+ Pokémon weak to the same type** just because they benefit from weather."
- **槽位含义（三条硬结论）**：① 6 槽 = **1 设置手 + 2 受益打手 + 2~3 补盲/联防槽**，是外部资料的**共识配方**；② **必留 1 只「不依赖天气也能打」的成员**（ER 对应「通用兜底槽/保底槽」）；③ **补盲槽是固定职责**（雨 → 草/电；晴 → 岩/地/水；沙 → 水/草/格斗；雪 → 火/格斗/钢/岩）。

## 3. 六人队的角色构成与配额

### 3.1 【官方论坛】各 archetype 的槽位配额（本文件最重要的一条）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/teambuilding-guide.3552468/（经 `_02` §1.4 登记，原文照录）
- "To wonder what set to run on a Pokémon is to build without roles."
- 配额表（照录）：**HO** 2–4 清场手 / 1–3 破盾手 / 0–1 破受手 / 1 撒钉手 / 0–1 除钉手；**BO** 1 清场手 / 2–3 破盾手 / 1 破受手 / 1 撒钉手 / 0–1 除钉手；**Balance** 0–1 清场手 / 1–2 破盾手 / 1 破受手 / 2–3 轮转或墙 / 1 撒钉手 / 1 除钉手；**Stall** 1 特耐墙 / 1 物耐墙 / 防强化 / 必备功能 / 视情况加轮转。
- **可信度提示（照录 `_02`）**：版主批评该帖 "far too linear"——配额是**检查表**，不是**公式**。
- **用法**：07 **不照抄数字**，只采纳方法论内核——「槽位先行、按槽位定义角色、每槽位有可判定的必要条件」。

### 3.2 【社区指南】槽位填充的**顺序**（本文件新增，直接决定 `buildTeam` 流程）

- **URL**：https://pokestats.cc/guides/sun-team-guide（片段核验通过）
- **原文四步**：
  1. "#01 **Lock in the Sun Core**"（先锁体系核心 = 设置手）
  2. "#02 **Choose 1-2 Sun Abusers**"（再定 1–2 名体系受益打手）
  3. "#03 **Add Speed Control**"（补控速槽：顺风/先制）
  4. "#04 **Counter Weather Threats**"（最后补「反制对手天气」槽）
- **同页三档骨架（含强度分级）**：`Tailwind Sun`（S 级，核心 Koraidon + Tornadus + Flutter Mane）/ `Hyper Offense Sun`（A 级，核心 Koraidon + Chi-Yu + Chien-Pao，弱点"No speed control; **weak to Tailwind and Trick Room**"）/ `Bulky Sun`（A 级，核心 Koraidon + Incineroar + Rillaboom）。
- **槽位含义**：**填充顺序固定为「设置手 → 受益核 → 控速 → 反天气/补盲」**；07 的 `buildTeam` 即按此四阶段实现（见 07 §四）。

### 3.3 【官方文章】五档 archetype 决定「哪些槽位必须有」

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/getting-started（经 `_02` §1.5 登记）
- 要点：HO 不评联防、评「清场路径」；BO 靠轮转招把破盾手安全换入、**保持场上无钉是刚需**；Balance 需要除钉；Semi-stall 只有**一个**耐久型强化手作唯一胜点；Stall 的胜点是**残余伤害**。
- **槽位含义**：**档位决定槽位集合**——HO 的槽位表里**没有物盾/特盾**，换成铺场/清场/先制槽。这正是「不该一套槽位打天下」的依据。

### 3.4 【官方文章】首发是独立职责

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/getting-started ｜ https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue8/leads（均经 `_02` 登记）
- **原文摘录**："A good lead Pokémon is one that has a favorable matchup against most of the opposing team and / or is capable of pivoting out in order to end up in a favorable position at the end of the first turn."
- **槽位含义**：「首发」是**独立槽位职责**；ER 无 Team Preview（`_02` §1.6）→ 首发槽按「最坏情况不崩」评分。

## 4. 进攻核 / 防守核 / 联防

### 4.1 【官方文章】组核四步

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue39/synergy-cores-teambuilding（`_02` §1.1）
- **原文摘录**："A good way to start when building a core is to select one Pokémon and identify its weaknesses and checks and counters. Then, try to find another Pokémon that covers these weaknesses in the best possible manner."
- **槽位含义**：槽位填充 = **先定核心槽 → 列弱点 → 填覆盖槽**；覆盖槽判据为**属性 × 特性双维**（ER 叠加 4 特性）。

### 4.2 【官方文章】核心规模与三种耦合

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue42/good-teambuilding-cores-ou（`_02` §1.2）
- **原文摘录**："The most common ways a core functions is by having the two team members eliminate each other's counters so one can sweep, having amazing offensive or defensive type synergy, or **by having one set ideal conditions for the other**."
- **槽位含义**：第 ③ 种耦合 = **「条件槽 → 受益槽」配对**（设天气 / 铺钉 / 降速 / 开空间），07 的「设置手槽 ↔ 体系核心槽」即此类。

### 4.3 【官方文章】联防的可判定定义

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue39/synergy-cores-teambuilding
- **原文摘录**："Synergy through typing is when two or more Pokémon are mutually resistant or immune to the other's weaknesses. This allows them to switch back and forth freely without taking much damage."
- **槽位含义**：「联防槽」**不是一只怪，是一条双向覆盖关系**；引擎判定用 `defCellTrio(t, w).opt == 0 / < 1`。

### 4.4 【官方论坛】进攻性冗余 / 防守性冗余（**正向**设计）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/teambuilding-guide.3552468/（`_02` §1.8）
- **原文摘录**："This is when offensive Pokémon have check (and coverage) that overlaps, resulting in the same Pokémon from the opposing team to be the response for both." / "Defensive redundancy is when a couple of walls counter the same Pokémon."
- **槽位含义**：**冗余是正向价值**——07 把「打点重叠」「防守双保险」都写成**加分项**，呼应用户「全部正向加分制」。

## 5. 队伍哲学：offense / balance / stall

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/getting-started（`_02` §1.5、§5.1）
- **原文摘录**："Balance is seen as a common middle ground between hyper offense and stall, where the team has a strong defensive backbone that can act as defensive pivots and strong wallbreakers that can punch holes through the opposing team." / "Stall is … defined through its main win condition being **residual damage** provided by bulky walls to wear down the opposing team slowly."
- **槽位含义（三条）**：
  1. **Balance = 骨架 + 开路人**：必须同时有「防守骨架槽」与「破盾槽」，不可互替。
  2. **Stall 的胜点不是任何一只怪，而是「残余伤害流水线」**：钉子槽 + 状态槽 + 回复槽 + 除钉槽**四槽齐备**才算完整。
  3. **HO 的槽位表没有物盾/特盾**——槽位表随档位切换。
- **【官方文章】破受三件套**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue18/stallbreakers（经 `_02` 登记）—— 挑衅 + 鬼火/剧毒 + 回复/削道具，**挑衅为核心**。→ 受队槽位表须含**破受对策槽**。

## 6. 钉子 + 轮转（VoltTurn）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/voltturn-in-ou
- **原文摘录**："Any type of hazard support is very appreciated by VoltTurn teams, as they often force the opponent to make many switches, and therefore, the hazards are often crucial to applying pressure and accumulating damage slowly on the opponent."

- **槽位含义**：「削道具」与「轮转」同属**进度手段**，并入同一槽位的评分维度。

### 6.3 【官方文章】中转两形态（快 / 慢）

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/articles/pivots-sm-ou

- **槽位含义**：「轮转槽」按速度分**两个子型**（快折返 = 侦察/压场；慢折返 = 安全换入），各给不同权重。

## 7. 戏法空间（Trick Room）队结构

### 7.1 【官方文章】TR 的核心矛盾：窗口短 → 必须够肉 + 够多手

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/dp/articles/trick_room_guide（全文精读）
- **原文摘录**：
  - "Trick Room teams need constant support, so having **two to three Trick Room users is almost necessary**. That leaves only **three spots for sweepers**."
  - "Anything over base 100 Pokémon is **risky to use inside of Trick Room**"（附速度分档表：base100/0IV/减速性 → 可超速 base75 档）
  - Checklist："**Have a few Pokémon that can Trick Room**" / "**Have enough power behind your attacks**"（推荐双刀破墙）/ "**Have a well balanced team**" / "You usually want your Speed IV to be as low as it can be, normally **0**"
  - "There is no point in trying to run a stall Trick Room team … **the only wall like Pokémon on your team should be the one setting up Trick Room**."
- **槽位解读（6 槽）**：① 首发空间手 ②③ 第二/第三空间手（互为保险、弱点互补）④⑤⑥ 低速高攻/双刀打手（0 速 IV）。

### 13.1 【关键反证】「严格角色分配」被官方论坛明确否定 ★本节最重要一条

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/gen-8-ou-looking-for-advice-on-my-first-no-hazard-pivot-team-specs-dragapult-weavile.3784200/
- **原文摘录**："This is a problem you are consistently going to have if you try to think it terms strict role allocation when building: **you do not need a special or physical wall, as much as you need counterplay against specific threats.**"
- **解读**：这是本项目能拿到的**最直接的反固定模板证据**——Counterplay 的单位是「**对具体威胁的答案**」，而不是「物盾槽／特盾槽」这类预设位置。v4.2 的 18×6 骨架若被当成「必须先填满这些位置」，正是本条批评的对象。
- **落到 v4.3**：槽位**不是前置的**；需求由**威胁与核心画像推导**而来（见 07 §4.5「需求清单生成规则」）。

### 13.2 【官方文章】核＝自足中心，其余成员「补核的洞」

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue39/synergy-cores-teambuilding ｜ https://www.smogon.com/xy/articles/synergy
- **原文摘录**：
  1. "Cores are groups of two or more Pokémon that have synergy through their typing, through checks and counters, or both. **Cores are often self-sufficient and are usually used as the centers of teams that are built around them.**"
  2. "**A good way to start when building a core is to select one Pokémon and identify its weaknesses and checks and counters. Then, try to find another Pokémon that covers these weaknesses in the best possible manner.**"
  3. "**When building a core, it is not wise to only pick Pokémon that have good type synergy, as it is more important to take into account the common threats in the current metagame and if your core is able to handle most of them.**"
  4. "Using cores as the basis of your team … A helpful teammate such as Talonflame is then **added to patch up the core's weaknesses** and allow that core to function better."
  5. "In general, there are two types of cores: **offensive cores and defensive cores.** … Offensive cores … aim to remove each other's checks and counters through their offensive type synergy in order to open up sweeping opportunities."
- **解读**：**「选 1 只 → 列它的弱点与 check/counter → 找覆盖者」**——这就是「需求清单生成」的原始形态，且官方强调「**不止看属性，要看环境常见威胁**」。第 5 条给出「流派」的最初二分（攻核/守核），但注意它讲的是**核的意图**，不是「队伍只有两种」。
- **落到 v4.3**：需求生成算法的主循环（07 §4.5 步骤 2–4）即本条 2 的可执行化。

### 13.3 【官方文章】三属性核：先立骨架，再用剩余槽补「能破核的具体宝可梦」

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/smog/issue43/three-type-cores
- **原文摘录**："These cores are great tools for teambuilders, as they enable them to **create a solid backbone for their teams without having to worry too much about specific threats**, and **then use their remaining three slots to fill in the gaps with answers to the specific Pokémon that can break the core.**"
- **FWG / Fantasy 核**（旁证 https://pikapedia.com/blog/the-art-of-team-building）："the most famous example is the **FWG (Fire-Water-Grass) Core**. This works because of natural resistances: Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water, and Water beats Fire. In modern generations, we see this evolved into the **Fantasy Core (Steel-Fairy-Dragon)**."
- **解读**：核负责「通用覆盖」，**剩余槽负责「具体威胁」**——这正是「需求**分层**」：**通用需求（属性覆盖）→ 具体需求（点名某只）**。v4.3 需求条目的 `priority` 应反映这一分层。
- **注意（口径）**：FWG 是**属性三角**这一**可用元素**，不是「队伍必须先摆火水草」；官方称其为 "rules of thumb"（经验法则）。

### 13.4 【官方论坛】需求驱动的逐步构建实例（非模板，是过程）

- **URL（实例 A）**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/teambuilding-guide.3552468/
- **原文摘录 A**："So you make a team centered around **Mega Medicham**. You decide you want to pair it up with **pursuit Bisharp to trap all the annoying fat Psychics that wall MMedi** … You decide to add a **Klefki for spikes support** … **Latias is added for a Keld switch in, as it easily beats the other 3, Zard-Y switch in, electric check, and healing wish support.** It seems pretty solid so far. **What does this struggle with? Well for starters, it's absolutely ravaged by Tflame** … **You also have no SR.** So what are some good options for the remaining two slots?"
- **原文摘录 A′**（同页 RMT 流程）"**Good teammates:**" …… 全篇以「第 1 只 → 它被谁挡 → 加谁 → 现在怕谁 → 再加谁」推进。
- **URL（实例 B）**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/reg-m-a-mega-chimecho-team.3783813/
- **原文摘录 B**：围绕 Mega Chimecho 建队，评论区按「它会怕谁」给答案："If you plan to build around it I'd recommend giving yourself **good tools to deal with stuff like zard, kingambit, basculegion, and delphox** … **Garchomp deals with Zard and Gambit, Primarina deals with Delphox, Incineroar and Sinistcha together handle Basculegion** …"
- **解读**：两例都是**「核心 → 威胁清单 → 答案清单」**，**没有任何一步在说「第 N 槽必须是物盾」**。实例 A 尤其证明「需求是在构建过程中**被发现的**」（先有成员，才暴露出 Tflame 与无钉问题）。

### 13.7 流派光谱：先选 lane，再决定「需要哪些成员」

- **URL**：https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/oras-ou-sample-teams-updated-10-2025-submissions-open.3762173/
- **原文摘录**："we have decided to update the ORAS sample teams to reflect **popular playstyles and archetypes** … **Here's a breakdown of 5 playstyles** aimed at helping newer players understand what terms like **HO or BO** actually mean"；样本按 **Hyper Offense / Offense / Bulky Offense / Balance / Semi-Stall / Stall** 分栏。
- **URL（定调）**：https://metamonsbuilder.com/guides/how-to-build-a-smogon-team
- **原文摘录**："Every consistent OU team fits **roughly one of these archetypes: Hyper offense / Balance / Bulky offense / Stall.** … **Pick one archetype before you draft a single slot. A team that's half stall and half hyper offense usually loses to both styles' best-prepared answers.**"
- **URL（lane → 具体需求）**：https://champteams.gg/guides/teambuilding-intermediate 的 archetype 表（Trick Room / Tailwind / Rain / Sun / Balance / Hyper Offense，每行给「speed control + win condition + support」）
- **URL（速度控制与流派匹配）**：https://champteams.gg/guides/battle-toolkit-great-ball —— "**Speed control is anything that changes who moves first, and it decides most games.** … good teams **pick the one that matches your Pokémon**. Slower, bulkier hitters want Trick Room; fast, aggressive teams want Tailwind"
- **解读**：**流派是「画像 → 需求」的中间层**：先有打法画像（lane），才知道需要哪种控速/哪种 win-con/哪种 support。这支持 v4.3 的**「流派动态推导」**（07 §4.6）——但推导方向是**从成员画像反推流派**（因为 ER 玩家先有精灵），而非先选 lane 再挑人。

### 13.8 【ER 专项】多特性系统使「特性池」成为第一决策变量

- **URL**：https://romprofessor.com/pokemon-elite-redux/what-are-the-elite-four-and-champion-teams-in-pokemon-elite-redux/
- **原文摘录**：
  1. "The **most important mechanic is the multi-ability system**. Pokémon can have several abilities active at once, which means **standard type-matchup thinking is not enough**. A Pokémon that looks like a normal counter may suddenly be much harder to break because of its **innates, defensive ability combination, or offensive ability stack**."
  2. "For Elite Redux specifically, **do not only ask \"what type is good?\" Ask \"what ability stack makes this Pokémon good?\"**"
  3. "A safe Pokémon Elite Redux Elite Four team should **cover roles instead of just types.**"
  4. "**Do not rely on a single Fighting-type** that can be removed by a surprise coverage move or priority."（→ 同类需求要有**冗余**）
- **解读**：ER 官方社区把结论落在「**cover roles instead of just types**」——**v4.3 的「需求」正对应此处的 role**；而第 1 条说明**为什么 ER 的槽位不能照抄原版**（特性组合跨属性改写了 matchup）。
- **口径提示（与 §8.2 一致）**：ER 的「特性池 n 选 1」意味着**同一只可扮演不同需求**（一只可同时是设置手与增伤核，如 `Charizard Mega Y`）→ 直接支撑「**多功能价值加分**」。
